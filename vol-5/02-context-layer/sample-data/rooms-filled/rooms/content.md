# Content

<!-- What you publish, for whom, and in what voice. -->

## Channels
- Instagram, 2 carousels a week, design teardowns
- A newsletter, every other Thursday, about 700 words
- LinkedIn, reposts of the carousels, low effort on purpose

## Audience
- Designers 2 to 6 years in, mostly outside the US
- They want to get better at arguing for their work, not at Figma
- They skim on a phone, standing up

## Voice rules
- Short sentences. One idea per line.
- Always a concrete example before the principle
- Numbers instead of adjectives: 11 screens to 5, not 'much simpler'
- First person, lowercase headlines, no exclamation marks

## Pipeline
- Idea goes in a note on my phone during the week
- Sunday: pick one, write the newsletter first
- Carousel is cut down from the newsletter, never the reverse
- Draft sits overnight before it goes out

## Never do this
- The words unlock, elevate, game-changer, delve
- Hook-then-nothing openings
- Threads of 12 posts where 3 would do
- Captions that just repeat the slides

## What good help looks like
- Cut, do not add. If a draft can be shorter, shorten it.
- Flag any line that would apply to any designer anywhere
- Keep my phrasing, do not rewrite me into a brand voice
